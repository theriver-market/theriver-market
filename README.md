# The River

The best place to trade and invest in tokenized US stocks, from your own wallet.

- **Buy stocks with USD stablecoins:** https://theriver.markets
- **Agent and developer API (no key):** https://theriver.markets/docs
- **MCP for AI agents:** `https://theriver.markets/mcp`

Base (USDC) and Robinhood Chain (USDG). Tokens are issued by their issuers (ST0x, Coinbase, Robinhood), not by The River. Not for US persons.
